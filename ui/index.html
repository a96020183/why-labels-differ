Every step, in order. Each piece of evidence came from one SQL query through Zetaris; click a query to see it.

    Night scan

    Every night the agents read EMA's catalogue of PRAC safety signals in order, 197 entries since 2019, and keep a ledger of where the U.S. label stands. The scan runs on the Meterless Markovian engine: each step carries a small, bounded state forward instead of re-reading the whole ledger, so the cost of a step stays flat however long the chain gets.
